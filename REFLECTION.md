# Project Reflection

## AI Evaluation Reflection

After completing our project, we asked two different AI models, ChatGPT and Gemini, to evaluate our work using the same prompt and criteria. ChatGPT suggested a grade of 89/100, while Gemini suggested a grade of 90/100.

The results were very similar, which showed that both AI models generally agreed on the strengths and weaknesses of our project. Both evaluations recognized that our topic was relevant and current, and that our presentation clearly explained how AI may automate specific tasks instead of completely replacing every job.

The AI evaluations also identified areas where our project could be improved. For example, they suggested making source citations clearer in the presentation and providing more detail about the relationship between automated tasks and the responsibilities that will still require human workers. Gemini also suggested explaining the different types of AI technologies involved in customer service, retail, and driving.

Overall, using two AI models to grade the project was useful because it provided an outside perspective on our work. However, we understand that AI-generated grades are only suggestions and may not exactly match the instructor's evaluation.

## How AI Helped During Our Research

AI was helpful during the research and preparation of this project. It helped us organize information about jobs that may be affected by AI, identify important areas to research, and structure the information into a clear presentation.

However, we did not rely only on AI-generated information. We used external sources and current 2025–2026 research to support the claims in our presentation. This was important because AI can provide inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete information.

The project showed us that AI can be useful as a research and organization tool, but information produced by AI should still be checked against reliable sources. We found AI most useful for organizing ideas, improving clarity, and receiving feedback, while reliable sources were necessary for verifying facts and statistics.
