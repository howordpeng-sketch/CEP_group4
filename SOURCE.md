# Sources

Research sources used for our presentation: **Jobs AI May Replace, New Careers Created by AI**

## Customer Service Jobs

- **Chatbase (2026).** *55+ AI Customer Support Statistics and Trends for 2026.*
  https://www.chatbase.co/blog/ai-customer-service-statistics
  Gartner's March 2025 research predicts agentic AI will resolve 80% of common customer service issues without human involvement by 2029. However, the same source notes Gartner forecasts that no Fortune 500 company will have fully removed human customer service by 2028 — supporting the idea that AI replaces routine tasks, not entire roles.

- **Klaviyo (2026).** *Will AI replace customer service jobs? What the 2026 data says.*
  https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/ai-customer-service-roles
  An October 2025 Gartner survey found only 20% of service leaders had actually reduced agent headcount due to AI, and nearly 80% of organizations plan to move agents into new positions rather than eliminate them.

## Cashiers and Retail Jobs

- **TomorrowDesk (2025).** *Self-Checkout Takeover: Robots in Warehouses Spell Doom for Retail Jobs.*
  https://tomorrowdesk.com/vigilance/self-checkout-takeover
  Between 2019 and 2023, the number of cashiers in general merchandise retail fell by 23%, even as total store employment grew by 3%, showing automation is displacing this specific role faster than others.

- **Netguru (2026).** *Automated and Cashierless Checkout: The Cornerstone of Autonomous Stores.*
  https://www.netguru.com/blog/automated-cashierless-checkout-autonomous-stores
  The retail automation market was valued at $12.2 billion in 2021 and is projected to nearly triple to $33 billion by 2030, driven by AI, computer vision, and IoT sensors enabling "walk in, pick up, walk out" shopping.

## Driving Jobs

- **ZetaCDL (2026).** *Will Self-Driving Trucks Replace Truck Drivers? (And Should You Still Get Your CDL in 2026?)*
  https://www.zetacdl.com/post/will-self-driving-trucks-replace-drivers
  As of 2026, driverless trucks operate only on a limited set of mapped highway routes and still depend on human drivers for city driving, docks, and last-mile delivery. The U.S. faces a shortage of more than 80,000 drivers, meaning demand for the job remains strong despite the technology's progress.

- **Kodiak AI, Inc. (2026).** *Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2025 Results.* SEC Form 8-K.
  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1853138/000162828026016456/kodiakai-20251231xearnings.htm
  Kodiak's driverless trucks had completed over 10,700 cumulative hours of paid driverless operation by early 2026 — real evidence that autonomous driving technology is moving from testing to commercial deployment.

## AI Replaces Tasks, Not Whole Jobs

- **McKinsey Global Institute (2025).** *Agents, robots, and us: Skill partnerships in the age of AI.*
  https://www.mckinsey.com/mgi/our-research/agents-robots-and-us-skill-partnerships-in-the-age-of-ai
  McKinsey's research found that current AI technology could theoretically automate about 57% of U.S. work hours — but this measures technical potential for individual tasks, not a forecast of job loss. The report argues the future of work will be defined by partnerships between people, AI agents, and robots, rather than full replacement of human roles.

- **Fortune / Yahoo Finance (2025).** *McKinsey explains why AI won't take your job, even though it can already automate 57% of all U.S. work hours.*
  https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/mckinsey-explains-why-ai-won-050100523.html
  More than 70% of the skills employers seek today are used in both automatable and non-automatable work, meaning most human skills remain relevant even as specific tasks shift to AI.

## Notes on Source Selection

All sources were published or updated within 2025–2026 to ensure the statistics reflect the current state of AI adoption. We prioritized primary research (Gartner, McKinsey) and verifiable company reports (SEC filings) over opinion pieces where possible.
