# AI Evaluation #1 – ChatGPT

## Prompt Used

Evaluate our CEP146 AI current-event project titled “Jobs AI May Replace.”

Our project explains how AI and automation may affect jobs such as customer service representatives, cashiers/retail workers, and drivers. We explain that AI is more likely to automate specific routine tasks than completely eliminate every job. Our presentation uses current 2025–2026 research and discusses how workers may need to adapt their skills as AI becomes more common.

Evaluate the project based on:

- Topic relevance and understanding
- Quality of research
- Accuracy and clarity
- Organization and presentation
- Use of evidence and sources

Give strengths, areas for improvement, and a suggested percentage grade out of 100.

## ChatGPT Response

Suggested Grade: 89/100

### Strengths

- The topic is highly relevant because AI-driven job displacement and workplace automation are major current issues.
- The project uses specific examples such as customer service representatives, cashiers, retail workers, and drivers rather than discussing AI employment effects only in general terms.
- The argument that AI is more likely to automate individual tasks than immediately eliminate entire occupations provides a balanced explanation of AI's impact.
- Current 2025–2026 research strengthens the presentation and makes the information timely.
- The project recognizes that workers will need to adapt and develop new skills as AI becomes more widely adopted.

### Areas for Improvement

- Include clear citations for important statistics directly in the presentation.
- Explain more specifically which tasks within each occupation are most vulnerable to automation.
- Include more discussion of jobs or responsibilities that are likely to remain dependent on human judgment, communication, and decision-making.
- A comparison between jobs, AI-automated tasks, and remaining human responsibilities could make the presentation easier to understand.
- The conclusion could more strongly connect the examples to the broader future of work.

### Overall Evaluation

The project demonstrates a strong understanding of how AI may affect employment while avoiding the oversimplified claim that AI will completely replace workers. The use of current research and concrete job examples makes the topic relevant and understandable. Stronger citation presentation and deeper comparisons between automated and human tasks would improve the project further.

**Suggested Grade: 89/100**
