# AI Evaluation #2 – Gemini

## Prompt Used

Evaluate our CEP146 AI current-event project titled “Jobs AI May Replace.”

Our project explains how AI and automation may affect jobs such as customer service representatives, cashiers/retail workers, and drivers. We explain that AI is more likely to automate specific routine tasks than completely eliminate every job. Our presentation uses current 2025–2026 research and discusses how workers may need to adapt their skills as AI becomes more common.

Evaluate the project based on:

- Topic relevance and understanding
- Quality of research
- Accuracy and clarity
- Organization and presentation
- Use of evidence and sources

Give strengths, areas for improvement, and a suggested percentage grade out of 100.

## Gemini Response

### Project Evaluation: "Jobs AI May Replace"

**Course Context:** CEP146 – Essential Tooling for Programmers / AI Current Event Project

**Suggested Grade:** 88%–92% (A / A-)

### 1. Topic Relevance and Understanding

**Strengths:**
- Excellent choice of target roles (customer service, cashiers, drivers). These industries are actively being reshaped by LLM agents, automated self-checkout systems, and autonomous/assisted driving technology.
- Captures the critical economic nuance: AI is primarily automating specific routine tasks rather than wholesale replacing entire job classifications.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Connect the topic back to software tools and developer roles. Brief mentions of how these systems are built or maintained would elevate the technical relevance.

### 2. Quality of Research

**Strengths:**
- Up-to-date scope referencing 2025–2026 current research.
- Strong thematic focus on reskilling and workforce adaptation.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Ensure the presentation cites concrete benchmark studies such as reports from the World Economic Forum, McKinsey Global Institute, or OECD.

### 3. Accuracy and Clarity

**Strengths:**
- Clear framing avoids exaggerated AI replacement narratives and instead focuses on practical workplace reality.
- High clarity in identifying which tasks are vulnerable versus those that remain human-centric.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Explicitly distinguish between generative AI and narrow AI/robotics so the audience understands the different technologies driving automation.

### 4. Organization and Presentation

**Strengths:**
- Logical progression from role overview to task breakdown, economic impact, and skill adaptation strategies.
- Structured breakdown makes complex economic transitions easy to understand.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Include a comparison slide or table mapping traditional job duties to AI-augmented equivalents and the new human skills required.

### 5. Use of Evidence and Sources

**Strengths:**
- Focused on contemporary data from 2025–2026.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Provide explicit source attributions on each slide or section rather than listing sources only at the end.

### Overall Evaluation

The project demonstrates a mature, realistic understanding of AI's economic impact. It avoids sensationalism and correctly highlights task automation and skill adaptation. To improve further, the project could provide greater technical detail about the software and developer tools enabling these changes.

**Final Grade Recommendation: 90/100 (A)**
